### English Version (`en`)

```text
Take a quick, sparkly arcade break anytime with BomBom Arcade right inside your browser popup!

TWO CUTE MINI-GAMES IN ONE POPUP
Star Dodge Runner: Guide BomBom with the arrow keys, A/D, or pointer/touch drag to dodge falling meteors and red warning lines. Time-escalating meteor waves keep late runs exciting! Collect mini stars and grab power-ups including Shield, Magnet, and BomBom Fever for bonus points and invincibility.
Sky Tower Stacker: Drop moving blocks with Spacebar, Enter, or a single click to build your tower higher. Land blocks cleanly for PERFECT combos—hit 5 PERFECTs in a row to restore block width and watch the sky transform from sunny meadow to sunset and outer space!

COLLECT STARS & UNLOCK 6 ADORABLE SKINS
Every 10 mini stars you collect turn into 1 Gold Star (with leftover fragments carried over across runs) for your shared BomBom Skin Collection. Unlock and equip all 6 cute friends—BomBom, Berry Slime, Rocket Kitty, Ribbon Bunny, Rainbow Cloud, and Gold Unicorn—each with its own look and tower block color palette!

OPTIONAL ONLINE LEADERBOARD, BRAG CARDS & CLOUD RESTORE
- Real-Time Top 15 Leaderboard: Set a fun 2–10 character nickname to post your high scores on the global Top 15 leaderboard.
- Shareable Brag Cards: Generate a 720×900 ranking brag card image with a clickable play link to challenge friends.
- Cross-Device Cloud Backup & Restore: Add a 4-digit recovery PIN alongside your nickname to back up your stars, skins, and scores via one-way SHA-256 hash keys and restore them on another browser or device anytime.

HOW TO PLAY
1. Click the BomBom Arcade icon in your browser toolbar to open the popup lobby.
2. Pick Star Dodge Runner or Sky Tower Stacker and press Play (or open in a full browser tab for a larger view).
3. Collect mini stars to earn Gold Stars and unlock new skins in the Skin Collection.
4. Optionally set a nickname + 4-digit PIN to compete on the leaderboard and keep your progress backed up.
5. Switch freely between English and Korean or toggle sound effects on and off anytime from the top bar.

PRIVACY-RESPECTING BY DESIGN
Offline play stays 100% on your device. If you opt in to the leaderboard or cloud restore, only your chosen nickname, game progress, anonymous Firebase Auth UID, and one-way SHA-256 hash keys (raw PINs are never stored) are sent to Cloud Firestore. No real names, emails, phone numbers, browsing history, or analytics trackers are ever collected.

SUPPORT & WEB PLAY
Play on mobile or desktop web: https://woosai.github.io/bombom-arcade/
Questions or suggestions? Reach out anytime at [YOUR_CONTACT_EMAIL].
```

## Privacy & Data Use (Updated for v1.1.0)

### Data Collection Disclosure (Chrome Web Store Privacy Practices)

- **Default (No Nickname Set):** Extension works offline; zero data leaves the device.
- **When User Opts In (Sets Nickname / 4-Digit Recovery PIN):**
  - Stores only **player-chosen nickname (2–10 chars)**, **Firebase Anonymous Auth UID**, **game high scores**, **Gold Stars & fragments**, **unlocked/equipped skin IDs**, and **one-way SHA-256 hash keys** (`claimKey` / `profileKey`) in Google Cloud Firestore (`bombom-db`).
  - Raw 4-digit PINs are **never** transmitted or stored anywhere.
  - **Zero collection of:** real names, emails, phone numbers, addresses, financial info, browsing history, website content, cookies, or analytics/ads telemetry.
- Aligns 100% with [PRIVACY_POLICY.md](./PRIVACY_POLICY.md).

### Data Use Certification
- [x] Data is NOT sold to third parties
- [x] Data is NOT used for purposes unrelated to the extension's core functionality
- [x] Data is NOT used for creditworthiness or lending purposes

## Review Notes

- Popup dimensions: `400×580` with optional full-tab view (`index.html?mode=full`).
- All runtime code—including the Firebase JS SDK (`firebase/app`, `firebase/auth`, `firebase/firestore`)—is bundled locally inside `dist/assets/` via Vite; no remote scripts or `eval` are loaded at runtime.
- No `background` scripts or `content_scripts` are declared; `storage` is the only extension permission.
