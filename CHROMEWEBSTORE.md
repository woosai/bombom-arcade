**Detailed Description** [REQUIRED]

### English Version (`en`)

```text
Take a quick, sparkly arcade break anytime with BomBom Arcade right inside your browser popup!

TWO CUTE MINI-GAMES IN ONE POPUP
Star Dodge Runner: Guide BomBom with the arrow keys, A/D, or your mouse to dodge falling meteors and red warning lines. Collect stars and grab helpful power-ups including Shield, Magnet, and BomBom Fever for bonus stars and invincibility!
Sky Tower Stacker: Drop moving blocks with Spacebar, Enter, or a single click to build your tower higher. Land blocks cleanly for PERFECT combos—hit 5 PERFECTs in a row to restore block width and watch the sky transform from sunny meadow to sunset and outer space!

COLLECT STARS & UNLOCK 6 ADORABLE SKINS
Every star you earn in either game goes into your shared BomBom Skin Collection. Unlock and equip all 6 cute friends—BomBom, Berry Slime, Rocket Kitty, Ribbon Bunny, Rainbow Cloud, and Gold Unicorn—each with its own look and tower block color palette!

HOW TO PLAY
1. Click the BomBom Arcade icon in your browser toolbar to open the popup lobby.
2. Pick Star Dodge Runner or Sky Tower Stacker and press Play (or open in a full browser tab for a larger view).
3. Earn stars during every run to beat your best score and unlock new skins in the Skin Collection.
4. Switch freely between English and Korean or toggle sound effects on and off anytime from the top bar.

PRIVACY FIRST & OFFLINE READY
BomBom Arcade works completely offline and does not collect, track, or transmit any personal data or browsing history. Your high scores, stars, unlocked skins, and preferences are saved locally on your own device.

SUPPORT & FEEDBACK
Questions or suggestions? Reach out anytime at [YOUR_CONTACT_EMAIL].
```
